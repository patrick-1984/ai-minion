Minion control

Which sessions may control sessions on other Workers. A master session controls a session on another Worker only when that Worker accepts control from the master's Worker (or from all Workers) and shares the session. Every other session is a slave session: by default all of them. User-managed Workers are also controlled by people and API keys, as before. Every click is saved at once.

A Worker has no master sessions until an administrator nominates one. The Hub also checks that the master Worker's owner may reach the target Worker.

How Workers grant control 
master session (Worker A)  ──►  Worker B accepts control from A (or from all)  ──►  B shares the session  ──►  A's owner may reach B  ──►  control
slave session (default)     ─✕  nothing beyond its own Worker

A session-bound grant follows one verified attachment. Name-only legacy grants trust the whole Worker account. Both grant types require the destination to accept control and share its session.
